- **New: `vibrato`** (technique, complete and ★: 4/8 · 5/10 · 4/8 · 3/6). Model: a vibrato is four independent numbers: width (half step = medium, whole step = wide), rate (1–4 cycles a beat, locked to the click), onset (straight first, then vibrato) and shape (even or widening); measured vibrato is written as bend–release pulses so width and speed are visible and heard, free vibrato as `~`. Where it goes is a degree on a string inside a pentatonic box (`spotDeg`), so every held note is in the key. Composer `vibRun(c, spec)` = spots (degrees × strings × boxes × keys) × kind (reference, measured, free, delayed, widening, compare, on a bend, double-stop, slide-in) × width × rate × hold. Foundations: hear the note then pulse it, narrow-fast vs wide-slow, measured pulses at one and two cycles a beat, every finger, landing notes (R, ♭3, 5) from memory, first phrases. Intermediate: rate ladder, wide (whole-step) vibrato, delayed and widening vibrato, vibrato at the top of a bend, bass strings pulled toward the floor, all five boxes, called degrees in box 4, a slow-blues solo. Advanced: triplet-locked vibrato, double-stop vibrato, slide-in vibrato, unison bends with vibrato, four keys, all boxes from memory, blues phrases and solo. Mastery: wide 16th-note vibrato at tempo, box 2 from memory, random key/note/width/speed/onset, capstone 8-bar study, two solos.
- **New: `phrasing`** ("Phrasing and space", subject, complete and ★: 4/8 · 5/10 · 4/8 · 3/6). Model: a phrase = a motif (a contour of steps on a scale "ladder" built from a box's fret window: minor pentatonic, or Dorian / natural minor for colour notes) + a development operation (repeat, sequence up/down, displacement by an 8th or a beat, pickup, new tail, cut short, inversion, augmentation, question on the 5th, answer on the root) + register and density + a landing on a tone of that bar's chord. Six named motifs (sigh, rise, call, turn, leap, cry). Composer `phr(c, spec)` = motif × plan (one op or a rest bar per bar) × register × density × scale × box × key plan × progression. Foundations: a bar of melody and a bar of silence, two and two counted aloud, singing the phrase in the rest, hearing the answer first, landing on each chord's root from memory, making the last note sing, a first call-and-response solo. Intermediate: say it / say it again / change it, sequences, displacement, pickups, the Dorian 6th as colour, each chord's 3rd from memory in box 2, a different change every phrase, question-and-answer, an 8-bar one-motif solo, slow blues with space. Advanced: a 16-bar solo with an arc (low and sparse, a dense peak two boxes up the neck, a slow landing), density on purpose, motif retargeted to each chord, motifs over a dominant blues, four keys, four boxes, a Dorian ballad study. Mastery: random motif/change/key/box, all six motifs from memory, a 32-bar arc at performance tempo, capstone 16-bar study, a Dorian vamp solo.
