- 2026-10-11 — **Stop wrong signature patterns reaching the player** (two so far: the Paul Gilbert lick and Eric Johnson's 5s were generic generators labelled with the artist's name). Four things, in order of value: (1) **Validator + CONTENT.md rule:** any lesson or unit that names an artist, or calls a pattern his/signature, must carry a `pattern` spec: `{ notesPerString, stringOrder, picking, sources: [urls], confirmed: 'primary' | 'secondary' | 'unconfirmed' }`; the validator errors when it is missing, and warns on `unconfirmed`. A test builds the lesson's tab and checks it against the declared `notesPerString` and `stringOrder`, so a generic group of N can't pass as a 2-per-string pattern. (2) **Naming rule in CONTENT.md:** a generic generator may not use an artist's name as its title or as "his lick" unless `confirmed` is `primary` or `secondary` (two sources that agree, one of them a primary one: his own lesson or video, or a magazine lesson he wrote or demonstrated); otherwise name it by its structure and say "in the style of". (3) **"Needs your check" list:** content runs end their summary with the artist-named lessons they built or changed whose `confirmed` is not `primary`, so the player can correct them before they spread. (4) **App:** a **"Report a problem with this lesson"** button on every lesson that writes into `Fretwork Coach research requests.json` (the file the content run already syncs from Drive) with the lesson id and a one-line note, plus an **"Unverified"** badge on artist lessons whose `confirmed` is `unconfirmed`. Content will add the `pattern` field to existing artist lessons as part of the audit queued today.
